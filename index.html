Suluhisho Youth and Elderly Empowerment is a community-based organization working alongside underserved communities in Mombasa and Kwale to improve livelihoods, promote green enterprise, and protect our coastal environment.

Explore Our Work Support Our Cause

Our Vision

A community where youth and elderly persons collaborate harmoniously to create sustainable livelihoods and inclusive development.

Our Mission

To empower women, girls, youth, and elderly persons with skills, opportunities, and sustainable solutions that improve livelihoods, strengthen communities, and protect the environment.

Our Core Programmes

Green Waste Management

Promoting waste segregation, collection, recycling, and composting of organic waste into high-quality fertilizer.

Green Skills & Livelihoods

Providing practical training in urban farming, composting, upcycling, entrepreneurship, and financial literacy.

Climate Resilience

Mobilizing communities for beach clean-ups, mangrove restoration, and climate-smart agriculture.
