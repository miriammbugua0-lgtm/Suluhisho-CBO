Our Vision

A community where youth and elderly persons collaborate harmoniously to create sustainable livelihoods and inclusive development.

Our Mission

To empower women, girls, youth, and elderly persons with skills, opportunities, and sustainable solutions that improve livelihoods, strengthen communities, and protect the environment.

🌱 Our Flagship Social Enterprise

Organic Fertilizer Production & Circular Economy

At Suluhisho, our core operational driver is collecting municipal organic waste and processing it into premium organic fertilizer and compost. This closed-loop model turns local waste challenges into agricultural wealth while providing sustainable livelihoods for women and youth across Mombasa and Kwale.

Waste Collection & Segregation

Community-led sorting and systematic collection of organic waste.

High-Quality Composting & Value Addition

Converting 240+ tonnes of organic material into nutrient-rich compost and fertilizer.

Reinvested Enterprise Revenue

Fertilizer sales directly fund local community programs and green skills training.

Our Core Programmes

Leading Focus

Organic Waste & Fertilizer Production

Promoting municipal waste collection, organic waste segregation, and processing organic waste into high-quality organic fertilizer through composting for urban farming to increase food security.

Green Skills & Livelihoods

Providing practical training in entrepreneurship, financial literacy, upcycling, revolving fund, and talent development.

Climate Resilience & Conservation

Mobilizing communities for coastal beach clean-ups, mangrove restoration, and climate-smart agriculture.
